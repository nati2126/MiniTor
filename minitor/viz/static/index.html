What you're seeing

Each node reports only its own links: a relay knows the hop before it and the hop after it, nothing more. This page combines every node's view, which is what a global passive adversary watching all links could do. Matching cell timing across links reveals whole paths. Tor doesn't protect against that adversary, and neither does MiniTor.
